# CS Glaze - Premium 2026 SaaS Redesign Complete ✨

## Transformation Summary

Your application has been **completely redesigned** from a dark, dull, student-project appearance into a **premium 2026 SaaS-grade product** comparable to Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Notion, and modern YC-funded startups.

---

## What Changed

### 🎨 **Design System**
- **Brand Colors**: Navy (#14213d) for trust, Orange (#fca311) for strategic accents
- **Light Theme**: 85-90% light surfaces (#fafafa background, #ffffff cards)
- **Typography**: Outfit for headings (replacing Montserrat), Inter for body text
- **Typography Scale**: Hero (64px), Page Title (48px), Section Title (36px), Card Title (24px), Body (18px)
- **Spacing**: 8px grid system with generous whitespace
- **Shadows**: 3-tier shadow system (sm/md/lg) for depth
- **Border Radius**: 20-24px for modern SaaS appearance
- **Glassmorphism**: Premium backdrop blur effects
